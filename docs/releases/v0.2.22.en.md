# Coopanion v0.2.22

**Coo can bring a picture back from the web**

## Added

- **Fetch one image**: the new `web_fetch_image` tool. Handed a picture link, Coo downloads it and returns it as an attachment — visible on the chat page, and Coo can read what is in it. The type is taken from the leading bytes, and one image is capped at 8 MB.
- **It stays off your machine**: a link to `localhost`, a loopback or a private address (10/172.16/192.168 and the like) is refused without a request, so a URL cannot be used to probe your devices.
- A fetched image can also be shown with `pet_say`, and stays in the conversation like any other attachment.

## Notes

- Thumbnails in the speech bubble are not part of this version; that is the next step.
