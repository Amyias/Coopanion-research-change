# Coopanion v0.2.23

**Pictures without opening a browser**

## Added

- **Image search**: the new `web_search_images` tool. Ask for a picture of something and Coo searches Pexels or Pixabay, takes a direct address from the answer, and brings the picture back with `web_fetch_image` as an attachment in the chat.
- Either free key works: put `PEXELS_API_KEY` or `PIXABAY_API_KEY` in `data/home/companion/.env` (Pexels wins when both are set), then restart the app.

## Fixed

- **The fetched page receipt was over the limit**: the 12,000-character default exceeded Core's 8,000 cap and the extra was dropped. The default is 6,000.

## Notes

- Keyless providers do not work here: Wikimedia Commons and Openverse time out, Bing answers with advertising and Baidu with a challenge page, so image search needs a free registered key.
