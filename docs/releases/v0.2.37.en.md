# Coopanion v0.2.37

**Upstream 0.2.20 and 0.2.21 merged: size the pet directly, click a line to skip it, a right-click menu of the fixed hover buttons, off the taskbar and out of screenshots**

## Brought in from upstream (24 commits)

- **Size the pet**: a size slider on the dress page and among the hover buttons; every figure and every favourite remembers its own size.
- **Click a say bubble to skip the whole line**: it fades, its voice stops and the lines queued after it carry on; questions and confirmations still need an answer.
- **A reminder sound when Coo asks you to confirm** (Habits → Sounds → reminder, on by default) and **a countdown in the status bubble while waiting**.
- **Hover hints** on hover buttons and the right-click menu (name and current state after about half a second), with entrance and exit animations for the menu, buttons and favourites wheel.
- **The right-click menu lists only the fixed hover buttons** (the same set hovering shows, at most six); an old install is told once after the update; the opening guide lets you pick the hover buttons.
- **Section jump bar** on the Habits page.
- **Coo can take a picture of itself on screen** (body only, no bubbles or desktop); **promises are kept in todo.md**, duplicate files are merged, and each batch of events is prefixed with its date.
- **Windows: "show on the taskbar"** (on by default) and **"stay out of screenshots and recordings"** (off by default).
- **Gemini endpoints take audio, video and PDF attachments** (18 MiB per request); Claude endpoints skip unsupported attachment kinds.
- Fixes: repeat answers after a handover, 400 after signing in with ChatGPT, stuck requests when pictures are rejected, favourites overwritten after a failed read, quitting under Wayland, extension event times off by eight hours, Minecraft reconnecting after a disconnect, a slow usage ledger, figure-pack failures, the opening guide pausing between steps, and more.
- Cortico submodule at v0.2.16: audio and video attachments stored by type, image fallback (400/413) in cortico itself.

## What this fork keeps

- Bubble picture carousel and lightbox, `/say-image`, `/api/looks`, page picture lists, the Bing fallback, the Node updater, the not-official notice and NOTICE.
- Where the two sides meet: **a click on a picture opens it enlarged, it does not skip the line**; the skip listener ignores `.b-pics`, and a click on the bubble afterwards still skips.
- The queue now uses upstream's `sayEnds` moved by id; pictures keep our `imageHoldSeconds`, and the old `pointedAt()` is upstream's `held`.
- The version number follows our own sequence.
