# Coopanion v0.2.30

**Upstream 0.2.17 merged: sound has a volume slider, saying stop interrupts computer use**

## Brought in from upstream

- **Sound volume**: the sound page gains a volume slider that covers the pet's speech too.
- **Saying stop**: speech on the talk key interrupts Coo's running computer use and its current reply; always-listening does not interrupt.
- **One key for talking and typing**: holding it past 0.3 s starts listening, and a double-click opens the typing box on the second release without a listening flash first.
- **Settings window**: the sidebar's Worlds follow the My World order; a World's avatar opens its info and drags the whole row; provider cards reorder by dragging and confirm before deleting.
- **Fixes**: the pet covering the App Store's Touch ID on macOS; old pointer movement mistaken for current input during computer use; the app quitting while antivirus or sync tools briefly hold a session file (it retries for about three seconds); a wrap-up reminder set no shorter than the wake-up limit never appearing.
- **Submodule to Cortico v0.2.12**: the WebUI matches the settings window's current version, World start/stop errors come back in the interface language, and a taken extension id logs a WARN.

## Notes

- The version number follows our own sequence; upstream's 0.2.17 notes do not replace the record of our same-numbered release.
- Windows updates work as before; macOS and the portable build are downloaded by hand.
