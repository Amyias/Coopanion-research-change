# Coopanion v0.2.35

**Upstream 0.2.19 merged: favourite looks switch above Coo's head, the right-click menu splits to both sides**

## Brought in from upstream

- **Favourite looks**: the dress-up page saves the current figure and outfit, reorders them by dragging; with the pointer on the dress button the favourites line up over Coo's head, and a click or a wheel that stops for 0.3 s switches, with a small entrance and exit animation.
- **Right-click menu split in two**: settings, pause/resume and quit on one side, every action on the other; Coo stays still while it is open.
- **Hover buttons can open My World**; click-through in Windows full-screen apps; the tray menu says show/hide in words.
- **Fixes**: Intel Mac packages carry the x64 native dependencies again (the launch-and-quit bug since 0.2.2); borderless full-screen games no longer cover Coo; a computer-use confirmation counts from when the bubble appears and earlier lines finish quickly; hidden and still pets stop redrawing; Coo can be clicked in a Wayland session; the console timeline no longer forces layout per record (about 12 s to about 1 s for 3600 records); settings layout in other languages and errors beside their controls; a World updated while running stays in place and keeps running its old version until a restart.
- **Cortico v0.2.14**: extension market tags and notes in all eleven interface languages, panels serving the running version's bytes, and more.

## Notes

- Bubble pictures and their lightbox, page picture lists, the Bing fallback, the Node updater, the not-official notice and NOTICE all survive.
- The menu avatar and name are gone with upstream, so the avatar file option had no consumer left and was removed.
- The version number follows our own sequence; upstream's 0.2.19 notes do not replace our record of the same-numbered release.
