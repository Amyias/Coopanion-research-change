# Coopanion v0.2.25

**Pictures in the bubble: page through them, click one to enlarge**

## Added

- **Pictures in the bubble**: when Coo shows a picture it now appears in the speech bubble, not only on the chat page. Several pictures page with `‹ 1 / 7 ›`.
- **Click to enlarge**: clicking a picture fills the desktop with it; arrow keys or `‹ ›` page through, and `Esc`, the close button or the backdrop bring the bubble back.
- **A bubble with pictures stays longer**: 6 seconds plus 2 per picture, at most 20; hovering pauses it.

## Notes

- The pictures stay on the chat page as before; the bubble's copies are valid for 10 minutes, after which the enlarged view is blank until the next send.
- Only the pet page changed; Core and the search path are untouched.
